Hello!

My name is Abdulboriy, and I was born in 2008. I live in Andijan, Uzbekistan, a city full of creativity and ambition. In 2024, I began my journey into the world of frontend development at Digital City in Andijan, where I immersed myself in modern web technologies and design principles. After a year of hard work and dedication, I successfully completed my studies in 2025. Throughout my learning experience, I discovered a true passion for building clean, responsive, and visually appealing websites. I enjoy turning creative ideas into interactive digital experiences that not only look good but also perform efficiently. I’m continuously improving my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React, and I’m always eager to explore new technologies that shape the future of the web. My goal is to become a professional frontend developer who creates impactful and user-friendly web applications. In my free time, I like learning new things, experimenting with design concepts, and staying inspired by innovative projects from around the world.
